Recommended examination sequence

  1. Confirm the authoritative version and the learner’s contribution.
  2. Read the purpose, methods, baseline and evaluation limitations.
  3. Inspect original evidence through the linked Drive or Colab item; permission may be required.
  4. Reproduce the relevant notebook in a fresh environment and record differences.
  5. Compare the final document against the rubric, references and submission record.
