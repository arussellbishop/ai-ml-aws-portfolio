Findings from the audit

Recent captures coexist with stale or failed sources. The inspected gold ledger needs a point-in-time review; the EWMA aggregate register needs reconciliation with individual run artifacts.

Engineering decision

Keep observation date, acquisition date and source provenance distinct. Preserve corrections and expose missing data before making comparative evaluation or model claims.

Limits and next steps

Resolve capture gaps, define baselines and leakage-safe evaluation, reconcile the observation history and verify independent recovery. No profitability, live trading readiness or forecast-quality claim is made.
