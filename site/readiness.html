The 100-point rubric

CriterionMaximum
Purpose and scope10
Implementation or research artifact20
Reproducibility20
Evaluation evidence20
Review package15
Reviewer access10
Limitations and next step5

Located source receives partial artifact credit; inspected implementation receives more. Saved replay reports receive partial reproducibility credit; a fresh clean rebuild receives full credit. Restricted links receive partial access credit. A summary is not a complete review package. Every dossier shows the exact awarded points and evidence.

The overall value is an unweighted mean across heterogeneous workstreams; it is not a measure of hours completed, product value or degree progress. Missing or unknown evidence receives no credit in that gate. Underlying project completion remains unassessed until its own acceptance criteria are checked.
