Recovered project and current coverage

The existing multi-PhD factory contains literature inventories, gap matrices and subject packs spanning drone autonomy, financial AI, education/privacy, graph AML, agents and energy. Its own missing-evidence report flags incomplete recent full text, empirical coverage, datasets and supervisor verification. The nominal RRIS Research Scanner folder contains only a starter README.

This refresh verifies selected primary records for robotics, quantitative evaluation and RAG evaluation. Education/privacy and AML remain in the catalogue with their coverage gaps; they have not silently become current systematic reviews.

Three hypotheses to investigate

Robotics: does dependence-aware confidence and staged reintegration improve collective reliability under correlated sensing errors and delayed messages? Compare against simple averaging and the 2026 Gaussian-belief-propagation study. Measure calibration, ranking error, recovery time and message budget. Start in simulation; physical validation remains a separate gate.

Financial AI: does a release-aware quality gate improve reliability under delayed or revised metal-market inputs? Compare against a fixed-age freshness rule using frozen temporal splits; measure leakage violations, abstention and stability, not just selected returns. Existing backtest-overfitting research prevents claiming generic evaluation discipline as novel.

AIOS/RSS: can provenance and freshness gates reduce unsupported claims in research briefs? Compare retrieval-only, standard RAG evaluation and a provenance-gated workflow against human-labelled claims. Measure citation support, stale-source rate, abstention, latency and cost. RAGAS already evaluates retrieval/generation quality, so evaluation alone is not new.

Search and review protocol

Search date: 20 September 2026. Queries included Bristol Edmund Hunt swarm robotics; Cranfield CRAN-0040/0041; AirSwarm 2503.06890; collective ranking environmental signals; RAGAS; probability of backtest overfitting. Include primary research relevant to the proposed mechanism and evaluation; exclude aggregators as factual authority, duplicates and unsupported summaries.

Current checks are abstract/metadata level for the cited preprints. Next: read full methods and limitations, search IEEE/ACM/publisher databases and backward/forward citations, record exclusion reasons, and obtain supervisor review. The accessible-source subset cannot prove that a gap exists.

For MSc review, map methods and results to the actual module rubric and final submitted version. For PhD review, add defensible originality, feasible access and evaluation design. No institutional assessment or admission is inferred.
