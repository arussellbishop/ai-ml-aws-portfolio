What the work demonstrates

The HMM dissertation’s saved reconciliation records 857 weekly observations, train-only preprocessing and prefix-only inference. It explicitly does not establish outperformance over gold buy-and-hold on a comparable window. This is historical replay evidence, not a fresh rerun here.

Finance Lab contains experiment and rejection registries, Monte Carlo diagnostics, Benjamini–Hochberg multiple-testing controls, cost modelling and portfolio diagnostics. Its final matrix closes the candidate family with no pre-holdout eligible candidate and records an untouched holdout. These are valuable negative results.

The inspected source-input-only rebuild report is INVALID because the permitted raw datasets and full regeneration pipeline were absent from that upgrade root. Portfolio diagnostic inputs are labelled diagnostic; approved portfolio: none.

What makes this useful in a professional review

Show the research question, timestamped input manifest, frozen specification, temporal split, baseline and transaction-cost assumptions before presenting a result. Retain rejected candidates and distinguish exploratory trials from confirmatory evaluation.

For the metals desk, reconcile the gold decision/input date inconsistency, EWMA aggregate history, stale FRED observations and failed CME capture. Release-aware freshness must distinguish unchanged weekly data from failed ingestion. A new dashboard does not repair those source issues.

Next demonstrable extension

Build an isolated research-only replay from licensed frozen inputs, regenerate ledger and report hashes, and compare a predeclared baseline under costs and stress scenarios. Report uncertainty and failed hypotheses. No live broker, wallet or order execution is part of this portfolio.

A source-backed gap hypothesis is whether release-aware provenance and abstention improve the reliability of market-state decision support under delayed or revised inputs. Novelty remains unestablished; compare against existing backtest-overfitting and point-in-time evaluation research.
